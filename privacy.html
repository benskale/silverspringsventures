Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Silver Springs Ventures LLC ("we", "us") builds and operates AI agents for businesses. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What We Collect

  • Business information: company name, industry, brand assets, and workflow details you provide during onboarding and discovery calls.
  • Integration data: when you connect accounts (social media, CRM, email), your agent may access and process data from those platforms on your behalf.
  • Communication data: emails, call recordings, and messages you send us.
  • Usage data: how you and your agent interact, including logs and analytics.

How We Use It

  • To build, configure, and operate your dedicated AI agent.
  • To generate content, schedule posts, and pull analytics from connected platforms.
  • To improve agent performance and provide support.
  • To communicate with you about your account and services.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secured cloud infrastructure. Integration credentials are managed through encrypted OAuth providers (such as Nango). We do not store plain-text passwords. Access is restricted to authorized personnel and the systems that operate your agent.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share it only with service providers that help us deliver our services (hosting, AI providers, integration platforms) under their own privacy and security commitments. We may disclose data if required by law.

Your Rights

  • Request access to or a copy of your data.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Disconnect any integration at any time.
  • Cancel your service and request data removal.

To exercise any of these rights, email ben@silverspringsventures.com.

Changes

We may update this policy as our services evolve. Material changes will be communicated to active clients.
